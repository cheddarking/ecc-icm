# Format: report.md

The shape ECC's deep-research skill delivers, kept so existing readers recognise it.

```markdown
# <Title>

## Executive Summary
<three to six sentences answering the question, confidence stated>

## 1. <First major theme>
## 2. <Second major theme>
## 3. <Third major theme>

## Key Takeaways
- <numbered citations on every line>

## Gaps and caveats
- <what remains unknown; what the sources disagree on>

## Sources
1. <title>, <author or site>, <date>, <URL>

## Methodology
<tools, queries, date range, what was excluded and why>
```
