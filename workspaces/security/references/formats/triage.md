# Format: triage.md

The human edits this file: delete a row to dismiss, change a severity with a note.

```markdown
# Triage: <slug>

## Findings
| # | Severity | Area | Location | Finding | Proof (input or steps) | Source (scanner or checklist item) | Fix direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Dismissed
| Scan item | Why it is not a finding |
|---|---|

## Needs a human decision
- <items the agent could not prove or disprove>
```
